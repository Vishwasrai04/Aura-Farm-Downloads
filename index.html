The app itself

A real screenshot of the current beta.

The tools are powerful. The work around them is scattered.

It isn't a lack of tools. It's the gaps between tools, information and files.

  • Context switching

    Work bounces between AI assistants, browser tabs, documents and apps, and focus breaks every time.

  • Scattered assets

    Images, video, audio and PDFs land in disconnected folders you have to hunt through later.

  • Lost knowledge

    Great answers, references and ideas get buried in chat history and browsing history.

  • Fragmented projects

    Research, drafts, decisions and files live apart, so resuming a project means rebuilding it.

An organized workspace around the tools you already use

Aura Farm doesn't replace your applications. It gives them a home, so you spend less time managing work and more time doing it.

Questions

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that normal?

Yes, for this beta. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns about new apps. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only install files you downloaded from this page.

Where is my data stored?

This version keeps your workspace on your own computer. Services you open inside Aura Farm are third-party sites you sign in to with your own accounts.

Which tools are supported?

Aura Farm works with supported web and AI tools. Support varies by service and may change, so not every site will behave the same way. Tell us which ones you want next.

Is there a Mac, Linux or cloud sync version?

Not yet. This beta is Windows-only and local-first. Other platforms and sharing are things we may explore based on your feedback.
