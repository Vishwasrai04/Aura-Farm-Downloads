The problem

The tools are powerful. The work around them is scattered.

It isn't a lack of tools. It's the disconnection between tools, information, and resources.

⇄

Context switching

Work bounces between AI assistants, browser tabs, documents and apps, and focus breaks every time.

▦

Scattered assets

Images, video, audio and PDFs land in disconnected folders you have to hunt through later.

✎

Lost knowledge

Great AI answers, references and ideas get buried in chat history and browsing history.

❖

Fragmented projects

Research, drafts, decisions and files live apart, so resuming a project means rebuilding it.

Meet Aura Farm

An organized workspace around the tools you already use

Aura Farm is a local-first desktop workspace for research, creation and organization. It doesn't replace your applications. It gives them a home, so you spend less time managing work and more time doing it.

What's inside

Four things that keep work connected

⌘

Integrated tools workspace

Open supported web and AI tools inside one desktop environment, and keep useful sessions where supported, with fewer browser windows to juggle.

▤

Projects and sections

Group work into projects and sections so research, references, drafts and related activity stay organized around a goal.

✦

Research capture and notes

Capture useful information from supported tool sessions, keep the source context, and turn rough notes into selected final content.

⬇

Asset and download management

Track and organize downloaded images, documents, audio and other files alongside the project they belong to.

Principles

Simple, and on your side

Desktop-first

A Windows desktop app with an embedded Chromium-based web environment.

Local-first

Your workspace is stored on your own computer. No hosted account is required to start.

Bring your own accounts

You sign in to third-party services with your own accounts, subject to each service's rules and support.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that normal?

Yes, for this beta. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows a warning for new apps. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only install files you downloaded from this page.

Where is my data stored?

This version keeps your workspace on your own computer. Services you open inside Aura Farm are third-party sites you sign in to with your own accounts.

Which tools are supported?

Aura Farm works with supported web and AI tools. Support varies by service and may change, so not every site will behave the same way. Tell us which ones you want next.

Is there Mac, Linux, or cloud sync?

Not yet. This beta is Windows-only and local-first. Other platforms and sharing are things we may explore based on your feedback.

How do I report a problem?

Use the feedback form above or email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com. Screenshots help a lot.
